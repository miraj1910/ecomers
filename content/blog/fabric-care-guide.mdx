Your clothes last longer when you care for them properly. Here's a practical guide to maintaining premium fabrics.

## Washing Guidelines

### Linen
- Wash in cool water on a gentle cycle
- Air dry to prevent shrinkage
- Iron while slightly damp for best results

### Wool
- Hand wash or use the wool cycle
- Lay flat to dry — never hang
- Use a专门的 wool shampoo

### Cotton
- Machine wash warm with like colors
- Tumble dry low or line dry
- Remove promptly to minimize wrinkles

## Storage Tips

- Use padded hangers for structured pieces
- Fold knits to maintain their shape
- Keep leather goods in dust bags
- Cedar blocks naturally repel moths

## When to Dry Clean

Not everything needs dry cleaning. Spot clean when possible and dry clean only when absolutely necessary — it's better for both your clothes and the environment.
