Building a minimalist wardrobe isn't about owning less — it's about owning the right things. Here's how to curate a collection of pieces you'll love for years.

## Start with Quality Over Quantity

The foundation of any great wardrobe is quality. When you invest in well-made pieces, they last longer, fit better, and feel more intentional.

> Buy less, choose well, make it last. — Vivienne Westwood

## The Capsule Approach

A capsule wardrobe typically consists of 30-40 pieces that mix and match seamlessly. The key is choosing a cohesive color palette and versatile silhouettes.

### Core Essentials

1. **A crisp white shirt** — the backbone of countless outfits
2. **Well-fitted dark jeans** — dress them up or down
3. **A structured blazer** — instant polish
4. **Quality leather shoes** — they get better with age
5. **A versatile coat** — your most-worn outer layer

## Material Matters

Pay attention to fabric composition. Natural fibers like cotton, linen, wool, and silk breathe better, age gracefully, and are more sustainable than synthetic alternatives.

| Fabric | Breathability | Durability | Eco-Friendly |
|--------|:----------:|:--------:|:----------:|
| Linen | ★★★★★ | ★★★★ | ★★★★★ |
| Wool | ★★★★ | ★★★★★ | ★★★★ |
| Cotton | ★★★★ | ★★★ | ★★★ |
| Silk | ★★★★ | ★★★ | ★★★ |

## The One-Year Rule

If you haven't worn something in a year, donate it. This simple rule keeps your wardrobe curated and ensures every piece earns its place.
